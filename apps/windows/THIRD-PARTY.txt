Netfish for Windows includes:

- mpv (libmpv-2.dll), the film player: https://mpv.io
  Licensed under the GNU GPL version 2 or later; this build (with FFmpeg and other
  libraries) is from https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake, where its
  source and build scripts are published.

- The Vulkan loader (vulkan-1.dll), from the Khronos Group / LunarG Vulkan Runtime:
  https://vulkan.lunarg.com. MIT and Apache 2.0 licences; see vulkan.txt.

- .NET (Microsoft, MIT licence) and the Microsoft Edge WebView2 SDK (Microsoft, BSD-style licence).

- netfish-tunnel.exe (WireGuard away from home) is built from Netfish's apps/common/tunnel with
  wireguard-go (https://git.zx2c4.com/wireguard-go, MIT licence), gVisor's network stack
  (https://gvisor.dev, Apache 2.0 licence) and Go (https://go.dev, BSD-style licence).
